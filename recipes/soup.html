German Potato Soup with Sausage

Photo by Nicole Russell from allrecipes.

Description

This German potato soup with sausage is a hearty, stick-to-your-ribs meal that’s simple to make and full of flavor. Potatoes, carrots, celery, and onions pair perfectly with smoky sausage in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 ribs celery, sliced
  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley
  • 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 14 ounces ring smoked sausage, sliced
  • 64 ounces chicken broth
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Melt butter in a large soup pot over medium heat. Add onion, celery, carrots, thyme, and parsley. Cook 5 minutes.
  2. Add potatoes, sausage, and broth. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer 25 minutes.
  3. Ladle into bowls. Season with salt and pepper. For a thicker consistency, smash some of the potatoes with the ladle prior to serving.
